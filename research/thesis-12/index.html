Science · Thesis 12

Science has an Umwelt too

Species-specific scientific access and representational convergence

The thesis

Human science reaches its results through a species-specific access system of senses, instruments, notations and communities, and objectivity is disciplined public control of that mediation. The same access analysis shows that a representational-alignment score cannot, by itself, attribute convergence to a shared world rather than to a shared route.

The argument in full: Chapter 13. Science has an Umwelt too. Every claim below is recorded in Register II.

Limitations and open problems

Philosophical scope

  • The access thesis is philosophical; its falsifier is conceptual. Its distance from Massimi’s perspectival realism rests on the biological layer and needs explicit argument in the text. (12.1)

  • Paradigm case studies are illustrative, not coded, and “sensory ecology” is used by analogy for communities. (12.3)

Formal results

  • The partition result holds for deterministic maps; the tolerance version gives a covering, not a partition. (12.4)

  • The quotient mathematics and the error-partitioning statistics are standard; novelty lies in application and framing. No worked dataset is given for the noise diagnostic. (12.4, 12.7)

  • Exact Blackwell comparison holds only in the finite case with a shared state space; profile coding has no validated reliability. (12.9)

  • The mathematics of the realist-step analysis is elementary; the contribution is location. An earlier draft’s path-sufficiency and fibre non-identifiability results are dropped in R1, which credits non-identifiability to Dalla Riva 2026. (12.4)

Representational convergence

  • The construction shows that gallery dependence is possible, not how often it occurs; the coding of eleven findings is the authors’ own and unreplicated; the authors present the decomposition as framework and design, not a new theoretical result. (12.2)

  • Prediction-grid rows are not exclusive; rows that agree on every available column cannot be discriminated; route independence must be documented, since datasets from separate groups can share provenance. (12.3)

  • Neither design stage has been run. Large-gallery evaluation may require recomputing embeddings; many-to-many correspondence needs multi-caption corpora whose availability at scale is unestablished; full factorial crossing of route factors may be impractical, and partial designs estimate local effects only. (12.10)

  • The niche-construction prediction is untested; tightening could also follow from shared scaling without feedback, and convergence alone is not a feedback signature. (12.6)
