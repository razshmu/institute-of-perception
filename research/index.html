Research

One question, followed into three fields

Take something usually treated as neutral background — the senses of an animal, the instruments of a scientist, the clock used to compare two systems — and ask what changes when it is treated as a participant.

The programme is set out as 181 numbered claims in 16 theses, each with what is new, the prior art, a test and what would refute it. Read the introductory volume · Browse all claims · See every falsifier
