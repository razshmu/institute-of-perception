Auditing a claim along the path it travelled

Claim auditing along the access path

The thesis

Error in empirical science enters at the step from what an access chain stabilized to the target relation a claim asserts. A claim-level audit locates that step, tests robustness against named artifact routes, and outputs the strongest residual claim the evidence licenses.

The argument in full: Chapter 12. Auditing a claim along the path it travelled. Every claim below is deposited in Register II.

Limitations and open problems

Validation

  • No validation study has been run for DACF, the microscopy record, the sensory-ecology audit, the attribution grammar or the residual-claim rule. (11.3, 11.4, 11.6, 11.7, 11.8)

  • Inter-coder reliability is unmeasured: disposition boundaries, dependency groups, target levels and “named invariance” rest on reviewer judgment, and the named-invariance notion needs a closed coding list. (11.2, 11.3, 11.6, 11.7, 11.8)

  • Coding burden and incremental value over standard synthesis are unmeasured. (11.2, 11.7)

  • The claim passport has not been tested with users. (11.4)

Evidence base

  • The location thesis rests on worked examples; the share of overinterpretation errors at the operational-to-target step is unmeasured. (11.1)

  • Worked cases were chosen after their resolution was known; they show fit, not predictive power, and one case illustrates without validating. (11.6, 11.9)

  • The eleven-study profiling reading is a purposive route sketch, not a systematic synthesis; claim packets are not reproduced for all eleven, and the decisive experiment is specified, not run. (11.10)

  • The pilot had one coder and a purposive sample; its counts are not a prevalence estimate, they shift with how mixed claims are split (a later draft has 14 records), and the location of each narrowing needs one check against the SI table before print. (11.11)

  • Published methods do not report every relevant dependency. (11.2)

  • The DESI figures reflect DR2 and will move; “defensible combination” needs a list declared in advance. (11.5)

Scope and novelty

  • Jurisdiction requires an access product, a target relation and a specific artifact route; formal proofs and purely descriptive claims fall outside it. (11.1, 11.6)

  • DACF’s novelty is architectural: it reorganizes established validity concepts. (11.6)

  • The span condition is linear and local; nonlinear or global confounding needs more, and sensitivity directions are rarely numeric outside modeled systems. (11.4)

  • The microscopy record is built for imaging; transfer to other assays is not shown. Phylogenetic-comparative claims are outside the sensory-ecology audit by design. (11.7, 11.8)

  • The Big Bang decomposition adds no cosmological result; close swap-stability ideas circulate in cosmology commentary, so novelty lies in the general operational rule. (11.5, 11.12)
