Evolution · Thesis 9 Core programme

What counts as one

Individuation format and the receiver cascade

The thesis

Species-specific sensory ecologies shape perceptual ontology, the organism-relative units, boundaries, events and relations first available for action, and perceptual ontology sets the entry conditions for proto-logical cognition without determining formal logic or mathematics.

The argument in full: Chapter 10. What counts as one. Every claim below is recorded in Register I.

Limitations and open problems

  • The predictions are proposed, not tested. (9.1)

  • No exponent has been measured in any signalling receiver; human exponents may not transfer. (9.2)

  • The volumetric argument fails at the projection stage: receivers see a silhouette, not a volume. The planar-versus-volumetric comparative prediction is withdrawn. (9.2)

  • The nine-paper audit is a purposive sample, not a systematic review. (9.2)

  • The paper scored 5.4 in internal review and needs rebuilding. (9.2)

  • An interpretive thesis; its empirical case rests on and. (9.3)

  • The underdetermination half is Tal’s; the added element is the receiver as arbiter. (9.3)

  • The flicker-detection versus event-count dissociation follows from the definitions and has not been measured in a species. (9.4)

  • The 40 Hz figure is schematic. (9.4)

  • Each side of the contrast is developed separately in prior work; priority rests on the combined formulation. (9.5)

  • Tal 2019, McConwell 2024, Kragh 2008 and Carey 2009 were flagged as not yet read in full. (9.5)

  • The effect of motivation on mind attribution is already documented; the novelty lies in the architecture and identification logic. (9.6)

  • Only a toy simulation exists. (9.6)

  • Precision and prior shifts are not separately identified from response bias without extra design. (9.6)

  • Heritable variation in partitions is not established. (9.7)

  • Population-level fixation of a recalibration is not established. (9.7)

  • The argument is conditional on both. (9.7)

  • Fish do not do mathematics; the claim is about which primitive requires more construction. (9.8)

  • No experiment has yet tested the predicted acquisition order. (9.8)

  • Difficulty matching across discrete and continuous rules is an open design problem. (9.8)

  • Chase outcomes are confounded by hunger, experience, terrain and motivation. (9.9)

  • Ranking reversals are also expected from non-sensory performance trade-offs. (9.9)

  • No test design exists. (9.10)

  • It does not imply that science is species-relative; the thesis says so explicitly. (9.10)

  • The register cited Chang 2005; Inventing Temperature is 2004. (9.10)

  • Evolutionary link to human logic remains a hypothesis (9.11)

  • Not yet run (9.11)

  • A working map, not a manuscript. (9.12)

  • The stage-3 integration claim rests on an unpowered equal-area null (MacLaren et al. 2004). (9.12)

  • Only one system is mapped. (9.12)

  • ‘Separability’ means independent control of behaviour, not existence or representation of the component. (9.12)

  • The bisection point is also a decision criterion, sensitive to response bias, reinforcement and probe distribution; the method needs validation. (9.13)

  • The formula assumes bisection at the midpoint of perceived magnitude. Animal timing data often place it near the geometric mean, which the formula yields only as beta -> 0. (9.13)

  • Never applied to a signalling trait. (9.13)

  • Not executed: digit-tip trajectories not yet obtained. (9.14)

  • The available mesh includes apparatus, so automated counts on it were rejected. (9.14)

  • One track from one species. (9.14)

  • 37 surfaces from nine localities; surfaces within a locality are not independent. (9.15)

  • An earlier band-pass result (‘up to sixfold, not an acuity effect’) is superseded and should not be cited. (9.15)
