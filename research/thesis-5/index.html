Evolution · Thesis 5

A trait is defined by whoever receives it

Receiver-relative traits and the effective phenotype

The thesis

A measured change in a receiver’s sensory-organizational mapping reorganizes the functional units through which existing variation affects action. It creates new dimensions of selection before matching morphology evolves.

The argument in full: Chapter 6. A trait is defined by whoever receives it. Every claim below is deposited in Register I.

Claims

  • 5.1
    Receiver-relative trait reorganization (RRT) Central thesis

    A measured change in a receiver’s sensory-organizational mapping (R, Π) reorganizes the task-relative units through which existing physical variation affects action, creating new dimensions of selection before matching physical traits evolve.

  • 5.2
    Enviro-Type and speciation rate Testable hypothesis

    Clades whose members differ in more heritable receiver-field configurations (Enviro-Types) speciate faster, after phylogenetic control.

  • 5.3

    Effective-phenotype formal chain Operationalisation

    Φ_eff = {U, T, Rel, Bound} is produced from stimulus S(t), body state B and history H through R, Π and Ψ, and action, consequences and fitness follow from Φ_eff.

    Extended-system unit test Operationalisation

    Six kinds of effective-phenotype change Terminological proposal

Limitations and open problems

Evidence status

  • No empirical test has been run; 5.4, 5.6 and 5.8 are designs, and the underground hand is a thought experiment, not evidence. (5.1, 5.4, 5.6, 5.8)

  • An earlier simulation for φ (91% detection against 17.5% false positives) was withdrawn after three code bugs; its headline was wrong by a factor of seven, and no valid simulation replaces it. No stage parameter has been measured in any receiver. (5.11)

  • The effective phenotype exists as a preparation dossier only; priority requires reading Reichert & Caves 2026 and Diaz 2025 in full. (5.9)

Assumptions

  • Heritability of reaction-norm parameters is assumed, not measured. (5.1, 5.3)

  • The t_2 step requires a persistent mapping, a fitness effect, no cheaper correction, and heritable variation; none is guaranteed. (5.3)

  • Mediation requires no unmeasured confounding of receiver and performance. (5.8)

  • The equivalence tolerance and the unit-test thresholds are preregistered, not derived; results depend on them. (5.6, 5.7)

Construct definition

  • Enviro-Type has no operational counting rule; 5.9 must supply one, and sensory-gene diversity is a proxy, not the construct. (5.2)

  • “Exactly three” components could not be derived (Synthesis Plan, 18 Aug 2026): a partition needs no metric. (5.4)

  • The definition of φ is a framework; its content lies in the identifiability rule. (5.11)

  • The symbol q in the formal chain collides with partition strength q of Thesis 6. The community-level extension is marked as extension. (5.5)
