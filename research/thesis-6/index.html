Evolution · Thesis 6

When one limb counts as many

Sensorimotor repartitioning and partition strength q

The thesis

A change in how an organism localizes and controls a body field creates selectable functional variation before an external-surface protocol detects a matching morphological boundary. Partition strength q measures that repartitioning as an individual phenotype.

The argument in full: Chapter 7. When one limb counts as many. Every claim below is recorded in Register I.

    Simulation operating range of q Application

    In simulation with 400 trials per individual, q &gt; 2 detects a true two-region field in 96.7% of individuals, with false-positive rates of 0.020 under one region, 0.093 under a linear gradient and 0.167 under a steep smooth sigmoid.

    Protocol-indexed units Terminological proposal

    Unit claims are indexed to property, protocol, task and purpose; external-surface, physiological, sensorimotor, behavioural and developmental protocols return different partitions of the same body part, compared as full partitions, not counts.

Limitations and open problems

Evidence status

  • No evidence yet exists for selection on q, for its heritability, or for later anatomical accommodation; 6.5 and 6.6 are designs. (6.2, 6.3, 6.5, 6.6)

  • The validation is simulation only and establishes nothing biological. (6.7)

  • Halteres test alignment among partitions, not an origin without morphology. (6.6)

Estimator performance

  • False positives against a linear gradient (0.093) and a steep sigmoid (0.167) are not low; designs must preregister a steep-gradient competitor. (6.4, 6.7)

  • At wide transitions the residual “detection” (0.13–0.16) is the false-positive floor, not detection. (6.7)

  • The threshold of 2 ignores the standard error of the ELPD difference; a per-individual SE must be reported. (6.4)

  • Measurement error in q attenuates the selection gradient; repeatability must be estimated first. (6.3)

  • The five criterion thresholds are not yet set. (6.5)

Numbers and constructs

  • Final figures supersede preliminary ones: the median 14.6 is the confound-arm true partition (14.64), 13.9 is the specificity arm (13.86), and the final run gives 0.895 at width 0.5%, not 0.85. (6.7)

  • Symbol collision: q here is partition strength. The same letter names the task variable in the effective-phenotype chain (5.5) and the ICML alignment estimand. ESM S6 uses a pairwise co-membership q over 12 elements, a different construct; its figures (0.915 / 0.042 / 0.157 / 0.217) do not apply to field q. (6.4, 6.7)

  • The name “relational individuation” is no longer used. (6.1)
