שינוי חושי יכול לפתוח התפצלות

השערת התפיסה הטבעית ואבולוציה ממוקדת-קולט

התזה

הברירה הטבעית פועלת על עיבוד תפיסתי תורשתי, ושינוי אחד בעיבוד הזה משנה על אילו הבדלים אורגניזם יכול לפעול על פני הקשרים. שינוי תורשתי בקולט יכול לפיכך להתחיל התפצלות אקולוגית, ומסלולים ממוקדי-קולט מוכרעים מערכת אחר מערכת לפי תנאי נטישה מוצהרים.

הטיעון המלא: פרק 2. שינוי חושי יכול לפתוח התפצלות. כל הטענות שלהלן מופקדות ברשומה I.

הטענות

הטענות מוצגות בנוסח ההפקדה, באנגלית. זה הנוסח המחייב.

  • 1.1
    Natural Perception Hypothesis (NPH) Central thesis

    Natural selection acts on perceptual processing (the weighting, integration and stabilisation of sensory signals), and a heritable change in that processing changes which differences an organism can act on across contexts.

  • 1.2
    Receiver-first evolution (RFE) Central thesis

    A heritable change in receiver sensory state, such as an opsin or odorant-receptor variant, starts divergence in mate choice and habitat preference while the focal cue field is held constant.

  • 1.3
    Perception Ecology research programme Central thesis

    Perception Ecology is a research programme that studies how heritable, species-specific perceptual processing shapes ecological interaction and adaptive radiation, with the organism’s internal perceptual state as its first component.

  • 1.4
    Scope conditions for receiver-first routes Specific extension

    Receiver-first routes are worth testing where three conditions coincide. Receiver-state differences are repeatable or heritable, they redirect a behaviour with ecological reach, and that behaviour changes the cue, resource or reproductive…

  • 1.5
    Receiver-side pleiotropy: sensory-biased magic traits Specific extension

    One change in receiver perception couples ecological performance and mate choice, so the pleiotropy of a sensory-biased magic trait resides in the receiver rather than in the sender’s trait genes.

  • 1.6
    Primate trichromacy as candidate receiver-first loop Testable hypothesis

    Primate trichromacy is a candidate receiver-first loop: an L/M opsin change improved red-green discrimination, shifted fruit choice, altered seed dispersal, and so changed selection on fruit colour and on colour vision.

  • 1.7
    Sensory-contrast restoration as conservation lever Testable hypothesis

    Where turbidity, artificial light or noise collapses the sensory discrimination that maintains divergence, restoring sensory contrast protects the diverged populations more directly than managing habitat area.

  • 1.8

  • 1.9
    NPH amplification formalism Operationalisation

    Because one sensory feature S enters the perception of every environmental trait (P_i = φ(S)·E_i), a small change in S shifts all perceived traits together and scales the selection gradient on S with the number of traits it touches.

    Gene-edit test of sensory-initiated divergence Operationalisation

    Edit one opsin, phototransduction or odorant-receptor gene in Drosophila or a fish, hold the focal cue field within a tolerance band, and measure mate choice, habitat preference and environmental modification against unedited sibling controls.
