איך מבחינים בין סיבה סביבתית לחושית

המשולש האקו-סנסורי: אבחון מסלולים באמצעות התערבות

התזה

sensory drive, הטיית קולט, בחירת בית גידול תואמת ובניית נישה הם תתי-גרפים מוגבלים של גרף סיבתי אחד של שדה הרמזים E, מצב הקולט Os ופנוטיפ האינטראקציה Oi. התערבויות של קיבוע, מיסוך, השוואת מפגשים והחלפת משוב מכריעות איזה מסלול הוליד התאמה בין תכונה לסביבה.

הטיעון המלא: פרק 3. איך מבחינים בין סיבה סביבתית לחושית. כל הטענות שלהלן מופקדות ברשומה I.

הטענות

הטענות מוצגות בנוסח ההפקדה, באנגלית. זה הנוסח המחייב.

  • 2.1
    Eco-sensory triangle Central thesis

    Cue-mediated trait-environment matching is represented on one causal graph of E, Os and Oi, on which sensory drive, receiver bias, matching habitat choice and niche construction are constrained subgraphs. Holding, masking,…

  • 2.2
    Used habitat as a collider Specific extension

    Where an organism is found is an outcome of preference, movement and sensory state, so conditioning analyses on realised habitat use manufactures phenotype-environment associations through collider stratification.

  • 2.3
    Minimal route model with structural zero Operationalisation

    A three-state model X(t) = [E, Os, Oi] in which receiver state reaches E only through Oi (the structural zero ∂E(t+1)/∂Os(t) = 0) generates environment-first, receiver-first, sorting and feedback regimes from one set of equations.

  • 2.4
    Cue-mediated magic-trait reporting standard Operationalisation

    Every empirical magic-trait claim reports four elements: the phenotypic coupling route on the E-Os-Oi map, the architecture maintaining the association (pleiotropy or linkage), the evidence level (covariance, functional duality, causal…

  • 2.5
    Route-priority test Operationalisation

    Receiver-led and sorting-led ordering are distinguished by whether prior receiver state Os(t) or a non-circular exposure proxy better predicts later Oi(t+1) out of sample under matched E, with the causal graph stated before model comparison.

  • 2.6
    Matched-field (flat-E) persistence test Operationalisation

    If Oi divergence persists while the focal cue dimensions of E are held within a declared tolerance band over the decision window, a current-environment explanation of that divergence is rejected for those dimensions.

  • 2.7
    Masking-rescue channel test Operationalisation

    Reversibly masking and then restoring one sensory channel under matched conditions, with sham and general-impairment controls, identifies whether that channel carries Oi divergence; it does not identify the route that produced the divergence.

  • 2.8
    Three-link criterion for receiver-initiated feedback Operationalisation

    Feedback counts as receiver-initiated only when Os predicts Oi under matched E, Oi durably modifies E or E_exp, and the modified conditions return to affect later Os, Oi, fitness or selection, tested with feedback-on/off blocks and…

  • 2.9
    Encounter-equalisation test Operationalisation

    Randomising settlement or equalising host, habitat, timing and encounter sequence tests sorting: matching or assortative mating that weakens while Os is unchanged was produced by non-random exposure.

  • 2.10
    27-regime factorial intervention grammar Operationalisation

    Holding each of E, Os and Oi high, low or free defines a 3 × 3 × 3 grammar of 27 regimes, of which the twelve two-fixed/one-free regimes are the clean local route tests.

  • 2.11
    Host choice as receiver-first sorting Application

  • 2.12
    Eco-sensory triangle SCM simulation Application

    A VAR(1) structural causal model of E, Os and Oi, run under nine clamp/vary regimes including masking-rescue (γ_i suppressed then restored), recovers causal parameters by cross-lagged SEM and uses E_exp = E × sigmoid(Os) as its readout.

    Route-family classification result Application

  • 2.15
    Experienced cue stream E_exp Terminological proposal

    The experienced cue stream E_exp is a derived receiver-scale readout, not a fourth node, and is reported either as an exposure proxy built independently of Os or as a receiver-space model that does not count as evidence for Os.
