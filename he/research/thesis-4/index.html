היכן נגמר הגוף

חלוקת גוף-עולם כהישג בר-סלקציה (EFS)

התזה

החלוקה התפעולית של השדה לגוף עצמי ולעולם שמיש היא הישג אבולוציוני של ארכיטקטורת הקולט. הסלקציה פועלת על הארכיטקטורה החושית, הגופנית, האינטרוצפטיבית והסנסומוטורית שמשרטטת אותה.

הטיעון המלא: פרק 5. היכן נגמר הגוף. כל הטענות שלהלן מופקדות ברשומה I.

הטענות

הטענות מוצגות בנוסח ההפקדה, באנגלית. זה הנוסח המחייב.

  • 4.1
    Experiential Field Selection (EFS) Central thesis

    Selection acts on the sensory, bodily, interoceptive and sensorimotor architecture that draws the operational body-world partition, not on experience or the partition itself.

  • 4.2
    Partition precedes metric Specific extension

    A distinction must be stabilized (EFS) before selection can tune metric equivalence within it (Adaptive Metric Perception).

  • 4.3
    Pre-partition and membrane-first origin Specific extension

    The partition originates in a membrane plus chemotaxis and reafference, before any perceiving subject; before it is drawn, the cue stream is not sorted for the organism into body, object, route, threat or background.

  • 4.4
    Organismal regulation preserves its own regulating process Specific extension

    Stars and organisms both regulate; a star’s feedback preserves a balance of forces, while an organism’s feedback preserves the conditions without which the regulating process itself stops.

  • 4.5
    Body-world boundary set by a temporal binding window Testable hypothesis

    Signals integrable within the organism’s multisensory temporal binding window are assigned to the body, so baseline temporal acuity predicts the visuomotor delay at which body ownership breaks.

  • 4.6
    Coupled repartitioning across action categories Testable hypothesis

    Under a matched cue field, a change in receiver state or architecture reorganizes reachability, defensive threshold, route, shelter, social spacing and body-near contact together, as one partition not reducible to a single latent state.

  • 4.7
    Body as first object: reafference mutual information Testable hypothesis

    The self-cluster is the part of the sensory stream with the highest joint mutual information with action-contingent reafference and interoceptive state.

  • 4.8
    Field grammar and three fingerprints Testable hypothesis

    Selection tunes the grammar by which an organism sorts its field into functional roles, detectable by target-standard cancellation, lawful propagation into derived judgements, and co-warp of body-anchored judgements by one body-scale term.

  • 4.9
    Conditional-independence null for repartitioning Operationalisation

    Coupled repartitioning is tested against the null that category outcomes are conditionally independent given measured internal state, detection and motor capacity.

  • 4.10
    Proto-body Terminological proposal

    The proto-body is a minimal self-model with three components: a boundary estimate, an internal-condition vector and a controllability map.
