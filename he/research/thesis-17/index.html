אובייקטיביות בלי מבט משום מקום

אובייקטיביות כאינוואריאנטיות חסומה

התזה

תוצאה היא אובייקטיבית במידה שהיא נשארת בתוך סבילות מוצהרת delta על פני נהלי גישה מכוילים שאינם חולקים שום רכיב, לאחר תרגום לפורמט משותף.

הטיעון המלא: פרק 15. אובייקטיביות בלי מבט משום מקום. כל הטענות שלהלן מופקדות ברשומה II.

הטענות

הטענות מוצגות בנוסח ההפקדה, באנגלית. זה הנוסח המחייב.

  • 17.1
    Objectivity as delta-robust invariance across independent access procedures Central thesis

    A result is objective to the degree that it stays within a declared tolerance delta across calibrated access procedures that share no component, after translation into a common format.

  • 17.2
    The regress stops where routes run out, not where evidence runs out Central thesis

    Route substitution, the operation that separates target from route throughout science, needs more than one route, so the question whether a target exists independent of all experience admits no direct comparison and no discriminating experiment.

  • 17.3
    Receiver-class bounded invariance (RCBI) as a rival explanation of scientific success Central thesis

    We propose receiver-class bounded invariance as a rival to the no-miracles argument: measurement-heavy science succeeds because its practices stabilise invariances within practice-fixed uncertainty inside human receiver-instrument…

  • 17.4
    Classical action-world Specific extension

    Organism-scale perception is tuned to decohered, redundantly recorded regularities, so it maps a classical action-world: the overlap of what decoherence makes publicly accessible and what embodiment exploits.

  • 17.5
    Error-relative corroboration Specific extension

    Agreement among routes corroborates an endpoint claim only against a named error, and only when the joint result discriminates the target account from that error by a feature not produced by the disputed pathway.

  • 17.6
    Measurement requires methodological realism, not physicalism Specific extension

    Measurement commits the investigator to methodological realism only; scientific realism, structural realism and physicalism are three further independent commitments, and the default slide from the first to the last is where the method’s…

  • 17.7
    Four layers of standpoint dependence ordered by correctability Specific extension

    Standpoint dependence in measurement has four layers ordered by correctability: unit choice, instrument bias, species-specific accessibility and conceptual framing; calibration removes the second, reduces the third and leaves the fourth…

  • 17.8
    Umwelt-stability is mistaken for reality Testable hypothesis

    Humans take Umwelt-stability for reality because Umwelt regularities are reliable across individuals, developmentally entrenched, and preserved under instrumental extension.

  • 17.9
    Three statuses of a framework: method, research programme, metaphysical proposal Terminological proposal

    A framework is a method if it only demands a specified access chain; it is a research programme if it changes the unit of analysis, causal model and predictions, and a metaphysical proposal if it also changes what counts as fundamental.
