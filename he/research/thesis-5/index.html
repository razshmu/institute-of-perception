תכונה נמדדת בעיני מי שקולט אותה

תכונות יחסיות-לקולט והפנוטיפ האפקטיבי

התזה

שינוי נמדד במיפוי החושי-ארגוני של קולט מארגן מחדש את היחידות הפונקציונליות שדרכן שונות קיימת משפיעה על פעולה. הוא יוצר ממדי סלקציה חדשים לפני שמתפתחת מורפולוגיה תואמת.

הטיעון המלא: פרק 6. תכונה נמדדת בעיני מי שקולט אותה. כל הטענות שלהלן מופקדות ברשומה I.

הטענות

הטענות מוצגות בנוסח ההפקדה, באנגלית. זה הנוסח המחייב.

  • 5.1
    Receiver-relative trait reorganization (RRT) Central thesis

    A measured change in a receiver’s sensory-organizational mapping (R, Π) reorganizes the task-relative units through which existing physical variation affects action, creating new dimensions of selection before matching physical traits evolve.

  • 5.2
    Enviro-Type and speciation rate Testable hypothesis

    Clades whose members differ in more heritable receiver-field configurations (Enviro-Types) speciate faster, after phylogenetic control.

  • 5.3
    Three-times model Testable hypothesis

    Receiver reorganization at t_1 changes the interaction phenotype at once without change to peripheral morphology; at t_2 the altered behaviour can change heritable variants, so receiver reorganization precedes and redirects morphological…

  • 5.4
    Three-component receiver-relative organization Operationalisation

    Receiver-relative organization decomposes into registered metric m, probabilistic attribution α and functional co-membership Π, supported only if the multicomponent model beats decision-only, common-factor, metric-only, attribution-only…

  • 5.5
    Effective-phenotype formal chain Operationalisation

    Φ_eff = {U, T, Rel, Bound} is produced from stimulus S(t), body state B and history H through R, Π and Ψ, and action, consequences and fitness follow from Φ_eff.

  • 5.6
    Task-equivalence criterion for trait origination Operationalisation

    Strong trait origination is credited only if an independently measured receiver change prospectively predicts a split, merge or boundary shift of task-equivalence classes, confirmed on held-out stimuli beyond sensitivity, criterion,…

  • 5.7
    Extended-system unit test Operationalisation

    A body plus tool, or host plus symbiont, counts as one functional unit only if the integrated model predicts held-out data better than the body alone, the link survives a change of measure, and the system fails at stated thresholds.

    Underground-hand thought experiment and combined causal test Operationalisation

  • 5.9
    Effective phenotype Terminological proposal

    The effective phenotype is the realized organization, at a declared level and time window, of the units, boundaries, properties and relations that a receiver-body-environment system makes functionally available, split into available and…

  • 5.10
    Six kinds of effective-phenotype change Terminological proposal

    Effective-phenotype change comes in six kinds, value, attribution, boundary, individuation, dimension and relation, and only changes that cannot be written as a monotone transform of a predefined variable count as strong novelty.

  • 5.11
    Organizational architecture φ as the candidate phenotype Terminological proposal

    Organizational architecture φ = (θ, κ, ψ, λ) is the set of stage parameters of a registration-grouping-labelling transformation that produces realized organization O = (m, Π, α), and φ, not O, is the candidate organism-borne phenotype.
