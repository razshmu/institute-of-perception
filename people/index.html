Shmuel Raz, PhD

Founder and Scientific Director

Shmuel Raz is an evolutionary biologist and a working researcher in clinical machine learning. He holds a PhD in evolutionary and environmental biology from the University of Haifa, awarded summa cum laude, and an Associate appointment at Harvard University's Department of Organismic and Evolutionary Biology.

He founded CVAid Medical, an AI stroke-diagnosis company, and is a named inventor on U.S. patents in stroke diagnosis, motion tracking and fall prediction. He has published more than 30 papers with more than 1,100 citations, and has mentored young researchers for more than twenty years: high-school final research projects, B.Sc. and M.Sc. students, and today secondary-school researchers in Crimson Education's RISE programme.

At the Institute he leads the receiver-first research programme, the Access-Path Audit and the Young Minds track.
