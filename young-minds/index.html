Young Minds

Real research, earlier than usual.

A founder-led research track for selected students aged 11–17. Not tutoring and not test preparation: genuine scientific work, supervised from the first question to a poster, a presentation or a manuscript.

How to apply

What students receive

  • Weekly scientific supervision with the founder, with clear milestones
  • An analyst and a statistician behind every project
  • The full pipeline: literature, study design, analysis, poster, presentation and manuscript
  • One primary academic output per student, and a selective submission route where the work justifies it

Research domains

  • Sensory perception
  • Self-reported health and wellbeing
  • Behaviour and environment
  • Digital health attitudes
  • Student self-regulation

The pipeline

From a first question to a finished piece of work

  1. 1Screen fit
  2. 2Choose a domain
  3. 3Frame the question
  4. 4Map the literature
  5. 5Run the method and the analysis
  6. 6Write, present, submit

Integrity

  • Students understand, present and defend their own project
  • Authorship only where the contribution is genuine and documented
  • No publication guarantees
  • AI may support the workflow but is never an author

An example of the standard

A Small Sensory Change, a Giant Leap in Biodiversity: a Receiver-First Evolution

Frontiers for Young Minds, 2026. The Institute's own science, written for young readers and reviewed by them.

How to apply

Admission is selective. Send:

  • A short academic profile or CV
  • Current grade, school and country
  • A note on interests and motivation
  • Any earlier project, competition entry or writing sample

Shortlisted students are invited to a first research session to assess fit, readiness and direction.
