Three tests that would decide the programme

The designs are specified and the predictions are written down in advance. We are looking for groups with the systems and the data to run them.

Sensory ecology · genetics

Gene edit, cue field held

Decides: whether a receiver change alone starts a divergence.

Cost: one edited line, one season · design in chapter 2 · thesis 1

Animal behaviour · song learning

Split-brood tutoring

Decides: whether a category boundary is heritable, and separates calibration from learning rule.

Cost: one breeding design · design in chapter 9 · thesis 8

Machine learning · representation

Stage one of the alignment test

Decides: whether an alignment score is stable enough to attribute at all.

Cost: no retraining · design in chapter 13 · thesis 12

The scarce resource

The limiting resource is measurements of what animals discriminate. Genetic data are plentiful and statistical technique is not the bottleneck. Compounds with measured genetic covariance and compounds with measured perceptual grouping do not yet overlap at a single molecule. A group that holds discrimination data, or can collect it, holds the key to the blocked test in chapter 11.

Who we want to hear from

  • Sensory ecology and neurogenetics labs with editable model systems.
  • Song-learning and animal-cognition groups with breeding colonies.
  • Machine-learning groups that measure representational alignment between models, or between models and brains.
  • Meta-science and reproducibility groups, journals, and teams evaluating diagnostic or AI claims, for the Access-Path Audit.
  • Funders interested in sponsoring a specific pre-registered test.
