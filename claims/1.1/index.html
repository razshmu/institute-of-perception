1.1 Natural Perception Hypothesis (NPH)

Claim

Natural selection acts on perceptual processing (the weighting, integration and stabilisation of sensory signals), and a heritable change in that processing changes which differences an organism can act on across contexts.

NPH places the target of selection in perceptual processing as well as in receptors. One heritable processing change alters the set of differences available for action in every context, not only the detection of one signal class. The shift propagates into foraging, mate choice and habitat use, and so into ecological interaction and divergence. “Environment” here means the measured cue field and the organism’s access to it.

What is new

That receivers are shared across foraging, predator detection and mate choice, so that change in one carries across contexts, is the received position in sensory drive (Endler 1992; Cummings & Endler 2018; Dell’Aglio et al. 2023) and is not claimed here. What NPH adds is the placement of the target of selection in perceptual processing rather than in receptors, and the naming of that placement as a route to divergence. The breadth of carry-over is treated as a quantity to be measured, not as a premise.

Prior art

von Uexküll 1909/1934; Endler 1992; Boughman 2002; Odling-Smee, Laland & Feldman 2003; Hoffman et al. 2015; Dangles et al. 2009; Cummings & Endler 2018, Current Zoology 64(4):471–484, doi 10.1093/cz/zoy043 (added); Dell’Aglio et al. 2023, Cold Spring Harb Perspect Biol 16(1):a041428, doi 10.1101/cshperspect.a041428 (added).

Test and evidence

Published as Raz & Breitkopf 2024, Global Journal of Ecology 9(2):132–145, doi 10.17352/gje.000106. Test: compare lines differing in one processing trait (an opsin or odorant-receptor variant) in matched cue fields and measure the breadth of behavioural change across contexts.

Falsifier

A heritable perceptual variant changes discrimination of its target signal class only, with no change in foraging, habitat use or mate choice under matched cue fields.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
