Evolution · Thesis 6 · Operationalisation

6.4 Appendage partition criteria

Claim

A functional partition of an externally continuous appendage requires convergence of five criteria, regional heterogeneity, dual localization, differential loading, differential action and perturbation-specific persistence, plus cross-validated superiority of the two-region model.

No criterion is sufficient alone. The criteria are partially ordered but are not a demonstrated developmental sequence.

What is new

Modularity criteria test morphological modules; these criteria test functional partition within a morphologically continuous structure.

Prior art

Wagner 1996; Wagner & Altenberg 1996; Klingenberg 2008.

Test and evidence

Design only.

Counts against

A claimed partition yields no out-of-sample or causal consequence.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
