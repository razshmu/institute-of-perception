17.9 Three statuses of a framework: method, research programme, metaphysical proposal

Claim

A framework is a method if it only demands a specified access chain; it is a research programme if it changes the unit of analysis, causal model and predictions, and a metaphysical proposal if it also changes what counts as fundamental.

The receiver framework of chapters 1-16 is a method, and it fits scientific realism, constructive empiricism, perspectivism and physicalism alike. Receiver-first biology is a research programme inside physicalism. It earns that status when the structure card separates partition change from sensitivity change in real data. The metaphysical options belong to chapter 20 and borrow no authority from the programme.

What is new

Lakatos (1978) defines research programmes and Kuhn (1977) places metaphysical commitments inside the disciplinary matrix. This claim adds a three-way status test applied to one framework, so the empirical programme neither lends authority to a metaphysical proposal nor inherits it.

Prior art

Kuhn 1970, 1977; Lakatos 1978; Longino 1990; Hacking 1983; Giere 2006; Massimi 2022; van Fraassen 1980.

Test and evidence

The structure card is the named test of research-programme status.

Counts against

Applied to real comparative data, the structure card cannot tell a partition change from a sensitivity change.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
