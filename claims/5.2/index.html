5.2 Enviro-Type and speciation rate

Claim

Clades whose members differ in more heritable receiver-field configurations (Enviro-Types) speciate faster, after phylogenetic control.

An Enviro-Type is a heritable perceptual configuration that sets how the organism partitions its field. It is the precursor of the effective phenotype (5.9). The testable content is macroevolutionary: Enviro-Type diversity within a clade predicts its speciation rate.

What is new

Sensory drive links sensory adaptation to speciation for single channels. This claim predicts a rate effect from the number of distinct whole-field configurations.

Prior art

Endler 1992; Boughman 2002; Seehausen et al. 2008.

Test and evidence

Phylogenetic comparative analysis (BiSSE/HiSSE-type or tip-rate correlations) of speciation rate against a count of receiver configurations, proxied by sensory-gene diversity.

Falsifier

No correlation between Enviro-Type count or sensory-gene diversity and speciation rate after phylogenetic control.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
