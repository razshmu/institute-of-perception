1.4 Scope conditions for receiver-first routes

Claim

Receiver-first routes are worth testing where three conditions coincide. Receiver-state differences are repeatable or heritable, they redirect a behaviour with ecological reach, and that behaviour changes the cue, resource or reproductive environment later met by the same or descendant populations.

The conditions restrict where a receiver-first explanation is admissible. Where cue fields strongly constrain receivers, environment-first sensory drive is the expected route. Where dispersal or turnover erases accumulated change, receiver bias or sorting is expected.

What is new

Niche-construction theory states when construction matters (Odling-Smee et al. 2013) but not for receiver-initiated cases. This adds scope conditions specific to receiver-first routes.

Prior art

Odling-Smee et al. 2013; Post & Palkovacs 2009; Endler 1992.

Test and evidence

Scope statement, applied to candidate systems in the Perspective drafts.

Counts against

Receiver-first dynamics appear in systems where receiver differences are neither repeatable nor heritable, or where the behaviour has no ecological reach.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
