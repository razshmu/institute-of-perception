Evolution · Thesis 2 · Operationalisation

2.6 Matched-field (flat-E) persistence test

Claim

If Oi divergence persists while the focal cue dimensions of E are held within a declared tolerance band over the decision window, a current-environment explanation of that divergence is rejected for those dimensions.

Groups are placed in matched arenas with drift monitoring and a failure magnitude declared in advance. Persistence shifts the explanation to receiver state, earlier exposure or unmeasured cues. Loss of divergence under matching shows that the current field carries it.

What is new

Common-garden and reciprocal-transplant designs (Clausen, Keck & Hiesey 1940; Kawecki & Ebert 2004) hold environment constant. This adds a declared tolerance band on the focal cue dimensions and states what each outcome licenses.

Prior art

Clausen, Keck & Hiesey 1940; Kawecki & Ebert 2004; Kawecki et al. 2012; Seehausen et al. 1997; Wright et al. 2020.

Test and evidence

In simulation, flat-E raised route classification from 44% to 64% (2.14).

Counts against

Divergence disappears under matched cue dimensions.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
