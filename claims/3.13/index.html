3.13 Causal-route controls for metric claims

Claim

Four control contrasts classify a metric shift as receiver-led, environment-led, sorting-led or feedback-amplified: matched external cues, reversible sensory-route perturbation with rescue, receiver-state versus encounter-history prediction, and feedback-reset versus feedback-enabled regimes.

The size-equivalence test shows that a metric shifted; these controls show which causal route shifted it. A receiver-led shift persists under matched cues, disappears under route perturbation and returns on rescue. Receiver state predicts it better than encounter history does.

What is new

Matching habitat choice and niche construction name environment-first and sorting routes. This design imports the author’s eco-sensory-triangle route separation into psychophysical metric tests.

Prior art

Author’s eco-sensory triangle; Edelaar et al. 2008 (matching habitat choice) (added); Odling-Smee et al. 2003.

Test and evidence

AMP §5 (causal-route paragraph).

Counts against

A shift that persists after reversible route perturbation, or that encounter history predicts better than receiver state, is classified as not receiver-led.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
