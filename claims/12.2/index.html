12.2 Alignment scores: generative sources vs measurement layer

Claim

A representational-alignment score depends on two generative sources (a shared target and a shared route) and on a measurement layer (gallery, correspondence, layer rule, statistic). An association statistic over a chosen item population cannot attribute convergence to either source.

The target and route can make two representations alike; the measurement layer cannot, but it sets how much of that likeness reaches the number. The route splits into data provenance, task constraint and inductive bias, which vary independently. A construction shows the measurement dependence: two systems encode the same two-scale latent structure faithfully with different precision allocation. Mutual k-nearest-neighbour agreement is then high on a cross-cluster gallery and near chance on a within-cluster gallery.

What is new

Cui et al. 2022 showed gallery-structure confounds in RSA and CKA; Liu et al. 2026 proved linear metrics mix content with format; Dalla Riva 2026 derived non-identifiability from the loss; Avitan & Golan 2025 showed it for model recovery. The claim adds one decomposition placing the Platonic hypothesis in the target slot, the Umwelt hypothesis in the route slot and the metric findings in the measurement layer, and splits the route into three testable factors.

Prior art

Huh et al. 2024; Bosch et al. 2026; Cui et al. 2022; Liu et al. 2026; Dalla Riva 2026; Avitan & Golan 2025; Gröger et al. 2026; Kornblith et al. 2019; Sucholutsky et al. 2025.

Test and evidence

Constructive counterexample (R1 section 4.1); coding of eleven published findings, nine of which sit in evaluation factors (R1 section 7).

Falsifier

A general result shows that mutual-kNN or CKA scores are invariant to gallery composition for systems that share a latent structure but allocate precision differently.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
