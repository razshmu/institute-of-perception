13.4 Target-space conservatism hypothesis

Claim

The capacity to register relations grows faster than the capacity to formulate targets, so early uses of a new instrument deepen established target classes, and major conceptual advances coincide with target reconstruction.

Relations are nested as warranted ⊆ targetable ⊆ registered ⊆ couplable. A relation can be recorded by a detector and never become a candidate variable or object: the unformulated-relation problem. The hypothesis predicts that growth of registered distinctions leads growth of target vocabularies, ontologies and claim types in instrument histories.

What is new

Instrument-as-extended-sense accounts and perspectivism do not separate registration from target formulation. The hypothesis states a comparative, historically testable lag between the two and names the gap as the locus of unformulated relations.

Prior art

Hacking 1983; Baird 2004; Humphreys 2004; Rheinberger 1997 (epistemic things); Massimi 2022.

Falsifier

In instrument histories or data-intensive fields, target repertoires (ontologies, annotation schemas, claim types) diversify as fast as registered distinctions from the first uses of the instrument.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
