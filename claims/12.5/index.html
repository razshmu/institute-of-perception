12.5 Route vs instrument substitution signatures

Claim

Under route substitution, a shared target predicts that a named, calibrated relation survives every route change given preserved competence, while an Umwelt source predicts selective change in one pre-named relation with a stable negative control; instrument substitutions move the score under every explanation and cannot separate them.

The shared-target row of the prediction grid is empty: no route factor’s removal weakens the relation. The restriction to route substitution follows from the construction in 12.2. An Umwelt claim must name the constraint, the relation, the direction of change and a negative control in advance. One that predicts sensitivity to everything predicts nothing.

What is new

Huh et al. read convergence as a shared target and Bosch et al. read it as shared ecological constraint, without a discriminating test. This claim supplies opposite, pre-statable signatures for each and shows why instrument variation cannot discriminate them.

Prior art

Huh et al. 2024; Bosch et al. 2026; Gröger et al. 2026; Dobs et al. 2022.

Test and evidence

Prediction grid (R1 Figure 4, sections 3.3–4.3); test design in 12.10.

Counts against

Route substitutions that keep competence intact but weaken a calibrated relation count against the shared-target explanation for that relation. An unchanged relation under a pre-named constraint change, with the control stable, counts against that Umwelt constraint.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
