4.1 Experiential Field Selection (EFS)

Claim

Selection acts on the sensory, bodily, interoceptive and sensorimotor architecture that draws the operational body-world partition, not on experience or the partition itself.

The partition sorts the field into own body and reachable, avoidable, edible, usable or ignorable world. It is a heritable, evolved achievement of receiver architecture. EFS separates online organization, developmental calibration and evolutionary stabilization, following Tinbergen. “Experiential” is operational and implies no claim about phenomenal consciousness.

What is new

Affordance theory and Umwelt describe the partition; sensory ecology studies single channels. EFS treats the multi-category partition as one selectable phenotype and names its heritable carrier.

Prior art

Gibson 1979; von Uexküll 1909; Tinbergen 1963; Dangles et al. 2009; Cisek 2019.

Test and evidence

The coupled-repartitioning test (4.4) against the conditional-independence null (4.9), then comparative tests of adaptiveness.

Counts against

The category outcomes are conditionally independent given internal state, detection and motor capacity; EFS then reduces to a descriptive synthesis.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
