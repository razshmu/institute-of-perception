14.10 Commensurate nested cycles

Claim

Cycles at adjacent levels (atomic, biological, planetary, galactic) lock at commensurate ratios, so geological and biological cycles correlate with astronomical cycles at those ratios.

The hypothesis joins internal dynamical times with nested cycles treated as gearwheels. It predicts equal-τ structural convergence and commensurate cross-level cycle ratios. Milankovitch forcing of climate is the established instance of one such coupling.

What is new

Milankovitch cycles and circadian entrainment are known single couplings. The claim generalizes them to a cross-level commensurability rule.

Prior art

Milankovitch 1941; Hays, Imbrie & Shackleton 1976; circadian entrainment literature.

Test and evidence

Compare observed cross-level cycle ratios against a random null with a declared tolerance and a correction for the number of ratios tested.

Falsifier

Cycle ratios across levels show no excess of commensurability over the random null.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
