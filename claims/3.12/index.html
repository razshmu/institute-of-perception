3.12 Drosophila size-equivalence falsification programme

Claim

Measure size-equivalence of neutral discs, threat-like silhouettes and feature-matched non-threat controls across ≥3 anchors in Drosophila, then test state attenuation, bidirectional selection on the normalized remapping parameter ρ, and upstream visual readouts.

Controls are matched for luminance, contrast, area, perimeter, centre of mass, expansion envelope and Fourier spectrum, within the tolerance. State controls include fed, starved and re-fed flies, locomotor speed, sex, day and octopamine manipulation. Selection acts on ρ, not on raw escape probability. Support requires all five go/no-go criteria.

What is new

Drosophila looming work maps escape circuits and escape magnitude. This design targets the equivalence function and selects on ρ.

Prior art

Klapoetke et al. 2017; Ache et al. 2019; Dombrovski et al. 2023; Evans et al. 2011; Cheng et al. 2019 (odor and octopamine reverse visual valence).

Test and evidence

AMP §5 (go/no-go rule); experimental design guide.

Counts against

An effect that appears only as higher escape probability or shorter latency, or that fails any of the five go/no-go criteria, is scored as not spatial AMP.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
