12.3 Paradigms as epistemic sensory ecologies

Claim

A scientific paradigm is a stabilized perceptual regime of three jointly necessary parts (calibration and access regimes, trained discrimination, communal validation standards), and paradigm change is typed by which vertex of an E/Os/Oi triangle moved first.

The paper reads Kuhn through the eco-sensory triangle: E is the domain of phenomena, Os the community’s trained lens, Oi its instruments. It sorts revolutions into phenomena-led, instrument-led and theory-led routes, with Rowland’s redshift, the bubble chamber, Woese’s rRNA and Copernicus as cases. Collider science is treated as niche construction.

What is new

Kuhn gives paradigms without a biological access layer; Hacking and Chang treat instruments and iteration. The claim adds a three-part operational definition of a paradigm and a route typing of revolutions by first-moving vertex.

Prior art

Kuhn 1962; Hacking 1983; Chang 2004; Massimi 2022.

Test and evidence

Illustrative historical cases; a coded case study with several raters is the test.

Counts against

Raters cannot assign historical cases a first-moving vertex with inter-rater agreement, or the three components change independently without paradigm change.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
