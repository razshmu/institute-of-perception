11.3 Route-relative robustness and dependency-diverse support

Claim

Replication counts against a suspected artifact only when it changes, bounds or independently tests the dependency that artifact route runs through; replication along the same laboratory, reagent, cell line or pipeline reproduces the artifact as readily as the target.

Independence is relative to a named artifact route, not a global property of studies. In cross-study synthesis, each claim component gets its own overlapping, non-transitive dependency groups. Each dependency is recorded as shared-unresolved, shared-bounded, materially changed, independently tested or not assessable. Support is counted in these terms, not by paper count.

What is new

Wimsatt’s robustness analysis, Stegenga, and Kuorikoski & Marchionni require independent means of detection; Stegenga shows independence is hard to specify. This claim adds a claim-level, route-indexed criterion and tallies cross-study support by dependency status rather than study count.

Prior art

Wimsatt 1981; Woodward 2006; Stegenga 2009; Kuorikoski & Marchionni 2016; Stegenga & Menon 2017; Lawlor et al. 2016.

Test and evidence

XMRV: repeated positive PCR findings shared reagent and cell-line contamination routes (11.9). Cell Painting route sketch (11.10). Cosmology application in 11.5.

Falsifier

A set of cases in which replication along a shared dependency exposed the suspected artifact as often as dependency-diverse replication did.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
