Evolution · Thesis 6 · Testable hypothesis

6.2 Selection gradient on q

Claim

Repartitioning becomes evolutionary through three staged links, functional variation in q, selection on q via performance, and response given heritable q, with a nonzero selection gradient on q after sensitivity, size, strength and learning rate are controlled.

Failure at an early link bounds all later claims. The machinery is standard Lande-Arnold analysis and the breeder’s equation; the new element is q as the trait.

What is new

Adds q as a selectable trait to standard selection analysis.

Prior art

Lande & Arnold 1983; Arnold 1983; Falconer & Mackay 1996.

Test and evidence

Multiple regression of relative fitness on q and covariates; animal model for heritability.

Falsifier

q is not heritable, or shows no selection gradient after controls.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
