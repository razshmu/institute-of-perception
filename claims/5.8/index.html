5.8 Underground-hand thought experiment and combined causal test

Claim

Under matched physical sources, two independently estimated receiver mappings yield different performance surfaces and selection gradients on the same appendage variants, and RRT is supported only if the receiver pathway adds out-of-sample prediction in a combined direct-plus-mediated model.

The underground-hand case is a model-generating thought experiment. It fixes the appendage and varies the receiver mapping. The combined model tests whether the mediated receiver pathway adds prediction beyond the direct morphology-to-fitness path.

What is new

Lande-Arnold selection analysis and path analysis are standard; the new element is a receiver-mediated path estimated independently of performance.

Prior art

Lande & Arnold 1983; Arnold 1983; Pearl 2009.

Test and evidence

Design only; the thought experiment is not evidence.

Counts against

The two mappings yield the same performance surface, or the mediated path adds no held-out prediction.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
