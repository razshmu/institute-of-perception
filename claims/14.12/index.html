14.12 Floor and sky: scale position fixes matter and space

Claim

Each system registers processes much faster and smaller than its own reference scale as stable matter and processes much slower and larger as a fixed sky.

The note derives the appearance of solid and of space from the ratio between an observer’s reference clock and the observed process. It predicts that measurement error grows with the rate gap between clock and process. Curves collapse only under the scale-appropriate clock.

What is new

Timescale separation and the Umwelt are established ideas. The claim links the appearance of matter and space, and measurement error, to one clock-ratio variable.

Prior art

von Uexküll 1934; timescale separation in dynamics.

Test and evidence

Measure error against the clock-to-process rate ratio; curve-collapse tests under alternative clocks.

Falsifier

No growth of measurement error with the rate gap, and no collapse under scale-matched clocks.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
