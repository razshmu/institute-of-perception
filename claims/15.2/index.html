15.2 Nested receivers in quantum measurement

Claim

Quantum measurement is a layered chain of record formation through six receiver classes, each with its own success criterion; the framework addresses record-basis selection and record efficacy, not unique outcomes.

The six classes are primary, pointer-forming, amplifying, terminal-display, biological and interpretive. Three questions are kept apart: which basis records form in, whether a record acts downstream, and why one outcome occurs. The framework answers the first two.

What is new

The von Neumann chain and quantum Darwinism already treat the environment as witness. The claim extends the taxonomy to recorder, display, uptake and interpretation stages, each with a separate criterion.

Prior art

von Neumann 1932; Zurek 2003, 2009; Schlosshauer 2005; Blume-Kohout & Zurek 2006; Scully et al. 1991.

Test and evidence

The experimental test is 15.8.

Counts against

Reduced receiver signatures fail to predict readout behaviour across detector implementations, leaving only a clarifying scheme.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
