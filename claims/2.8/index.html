2.8 Three-link criterion for receiver-initiated feedback

Claim

Feedback counts as receiver-initiated only when Os predicts Oi under matched E, Oi durably modifies E or E_exp, and the modified conditions return to affect later Os, Oi, fitness or selection, tested with feedback-on/off blocks and standardised pulses.

Feedback is an experimentally toggled regime, not a default label. A pulse-response signature separates open from closed loops: in a closed loop the effect of a pulse persists or grows after the perturbation ends. This is the test protocol for the motif in 1.2.

What is new

Press and pulse perturbations (Bender, Case & Gilpin 1984) and eco-evolutionary feedback experiments (Post & Palkovacs 2009; Bassar et al. 2010) exist. This adds a receiver-initiation criterion and a durability requirement.

Prior art

Bender, Case & Gilpin 1984; Post & Palkovacs 2009; Bassar et al. 2010; Odling-Smee et al. 2013.

Test and evidence

In simulation, feedback emergence and post-pulse persistence raised classification from 77% to 83% (2.14).

Counts against

E diverges at similar rates under feedback-on and feedback-off blocks.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
