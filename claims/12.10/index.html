12.10 The realist step in the Platonic Representation Hypothesis

Claim

The realist reading of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis passes through one step, from statistics of observations to statistics of the world, which its authors prove only for bijective, deterministic observation; the reported scores estimate an evaluation-batch quantity that the theorem never connects to its training distribution.

Huh et al. show that, for contrastive learners with bijective observation, a representation can express the pointwise mutual information kernel of the co-occurrence distribution. They state that the argument does not hold for lossy or stochastic observation. Real observation is lossy, so the realist reading needs an argument the theorem does not supply. The theorem also concerns the generative co-occurrence stream, while scores are finite-sample estimates on held-out batches, and the paper does not relate the two.

What is new

Huh et al. 2024 state the bijection limit; Dalla Riva 2026 derives non-identifiability of world structure from the loss. This claim locates the realist step exactly and names the missing training-to-evaluation link as a second, separate gap.

Prior art

Huh et al. 2024; Dalla Riva 2026; Koepke et al. 2026.

Test and evidence

Textual analysis of Huh et al. 2024, Section 4 and limitations (R1 sections 3.1–3.2).

Falsifier

A demonstration shows that, under realistic lossy observation, the learned kernel still identifies world statistics, and evaluation-batch scores consistently estimate the training kernel.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
