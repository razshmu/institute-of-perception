3.18 Label-mediated transfer of population bias to a classifier

Claim

In simulation, a per-pixel classifier trained on segmentation labels from an evolved population carries that population’s measurement shift: area bias 0.30 and 0.13 for two population-trained classifiers against 0.79 for a ground-truth-trained control.

Each population labels pixels whose perceived intensity exceeds its own detection threshold. Logistic classifiers trained on these labels are tested on held-out rod images. The population-trained classifiers differ from the control in the direction of the populations’ rod-length underestimation (permutation p ≈ 0.04 for population B, five seeds).

What is new

Label bias in machine learning is established. The addition is a simulated route from selected sensory thresholds, through labels, into a trained tool.

Prior art

Annotation-bias and label-noise literature, e.g. Frénay & Verleysen 2014 (added).

Test and evidence

Simulation study, Pass 5.

Counts against

If population-trained and ground-truth-trained classifiers show the same area bias across more seeds, the transfer is not supported.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
