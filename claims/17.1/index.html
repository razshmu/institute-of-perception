17.1 Objectivity as delta-robust invariance across independent access procedures

Claim

A result is objective to the degree that it stays within a declared tolerance delta across calibrated access procedures that share no component, after translation into a common format.

Objectivity is a graded property of results, not a view from nowhere. A family of procedures supports a result when their translated outputs agree within delta and their error sources differ. Agreement that rests on a shared sensor, software library, reference calibration or statistical assumption is a shared artefact and earns no objectivity. Agreement becomes objective only when calibration, intervention, prediction, replication and cross-instrument comparison constrain it.

What is new

Robustness analysis (Wimsatt 1981; Woodward 2006) and perspectival realism (Giere 2006; Massimi 2018, 2022) already treat objectivity as stability across independent means of access. Tal (2016, 2017) already makes calibration model-based. This claim adds an explicit delta-robustness condition over translated procedure outputs and an operational exclusion rule: any shared component voids the independence credit. The exclusion is relative to a declared audit boundary. Absolute procedural independence cannot be verified, because two procedures may share assumptions, reference standards, training data or conceptual categories while sharing no physical component; the claim is therefore a practical criterion for strengthened objectivity, not a general definition.

Prior art

Wimsatt 1981 (robustness); Woodward 2006; Giere 2006; Massimi 2018, 2022; Tal 2016, 2017; Longino 1990; Perrin 1913 as the canonical case.

Test and evidence

Formal definition with a delta-robust condition, tau_i translation and d_N distance. Applied in the claim-passport rows ‘traceability’ and ‘shared failure points’.

Falsifier

Procedures that share no audited component converge within delta on results later overturned at a rate no lower than procedures that share components.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
