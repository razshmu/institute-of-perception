14.9 Disk galaxies saturate after a fixed number of orbits

Claim

Disk galaxy morphology and bar strength reach a stable, saturated state after about 10–20 internal orbital periods at a characteristic radius, independent of galaxy mass.

The paper extends internal dynamical age to disks, measuring age in orbits at R25 or a few scale lengths. It argues that bars, disk thickness and pseudobulges plateau after the threshold. This is the disk counterpart of 14.7, with orbital count as the clock.

What is new

Secular-evolution work (Kormendy & Kennicutt 2004; Athanassoula) describes bar and pseudobulge growth in physical time. The claim adds orbit count as the clock and a mass-independent saturation threshold with named observables.

Prior art

Toomre 1964; Athanassoula 2003; Kormendy & Kennicutt 2004.

Test and evidence

Matched disk samples or simulations binned by orbit count N_orb at a fixed characteristic radius.

Falsifier

Bar strength, disk thickness or bulge-to-disk ratio keep trending with N_orb above 20 in matched samples.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
