17.5 Error-relative corroboration

Claim

Agreement among routes corroborates an endpoint claim only against a named error, and only when the joint result discriminates the target account from that error by a feature not produced by the disputed pathway.

ACCESS assigns ‘cross-route corroborated’ only after the claim is aligned or bridged, route uncertainties are compatible and dependencies are documented. The status is always written as corroborated against error e under dependency model D and scope S. Corroboration against one alternative never becomes unqualified confirmation of a theory.

What is new

Triangulation and robustness analysis treat convergence of independent methods as support. This makes corroboration an error-indexed status gated by estimand alignment and a registered error set.

Prior art

Munafo & Davey Smith triangulation; Wimsatt robustness; Kuorikoski et al. robustness; Stegenga evidential diversity

Test and evidence

Worked reconstruction of Pundamilia sensory-drive evidence.

Counts against

Routes that discriminate a named error only by a feature the disputed pathway produces still reliably predict later adjudication.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
