Evolution · Thesis 5 · Testable hypothesis

5.3 Three-times model

Claim

Receiver reorganization at t_1 changes the interaction phenotype at once without change to peripheral morphology; at t_2 the altered behaviour can change heritable variants, so receiver reorganization precedes and redirects morphological evolution.

At t_0 stands the ancestral system. At t_1 the receiver reorganizes, and the same body acts through new units. At t_2 selection on the new interaction shifts heritable morphology.

What is new

Behaviour-first and plasticity-first evolution state that behaviour leads; this model locates the lead in receiver reorganization specifically.

Prior art

Baldwin 1896; Mayr 1963; West-Eberhard 2003; Levis & Pfennig 2016.

Test and evidence

Experimental evolution: impose a receiver change and track morphology across generations.

Falsifier

Receiver reorganization never precedes morphological change in lineages where both are tracked.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.

Discussed in

Thesis 5: Chapter 6. A trait is defined by whoever receives it
