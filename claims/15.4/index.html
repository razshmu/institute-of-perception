15.4 Single-core multi-port perception

Claim

One inferential core coupled to several sensory ports out-predicts independent per-port models per unit complexity and shows cross-port compensation proportional to port precision.

We extend interface theory from single channels to one core with several ports. Ports share upstream structure and keep local renderings. Selection then acts on the architecture, not on veridicality.

What is new

Precision-weighted cue combination (Ernst & Banks 2002) and causal inference (Körding et al. 2007) already predict precision-weighted compensation. The claim adds the architectural comparison against per-port models within interface-theory simulations.

Prior art

Hoffman, Singh & Prakash 2015; Mark et al. 2010; Ernst & Banks 2002; Körding et al. 2007; Friston (active inference).

Test and evidence

Evolutionary game simulations comparing single-core multi-port agents with per-port agents at matched complexity.

Falsifier

Per-port agents match single-core agents in fitness at matched complexity, or compensation is not precision-proportional.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
