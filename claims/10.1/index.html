10.1 Receiver-Calibration Selection

Claim

Heritable variation in a few receiver-calibration parameters θ (spectral, temporal, gain) changes many receiver-mediated interactions at once, so selection from any of them loads onto the same parameters: β_θ = Σ_j (∂ln w/∂y_j)(∂y_j/∂θ).

Each ecological target x_j is registered as y_j = R(x_j; θ). The signature of the coupling is a receiver Jacobian J_θ = ∂y/∂θ that is dense (many interactions affected) yet low-rank (few independent parameters). Density and rank are logically independent, and the claim is their combination. H1 follows: genetic effects across a battery of receiver-mediated tasks occupy a low-dimensional subspace that predicts held-out tasks.

What is new

The qualitative coupling is sensory drive, and the cross-context correlated-response experiment is Fuller, Houle & Travis 2005. The dimensionality estimator is Hine & Blows 2006, and low-rank G is an established empirical pattern for signaller-side trait panels. What is new is the counting move applied to the receiver: if a few calibration parameters mediate n distinct tasks, the additive genetic covariance matrix estimated across a panel of n receiver-mediated behavioural assays should have effective dimensionality set by the number of parameters and not by n. G has not been estimated across a receiver-task panel in any species, so the prediction stands untested.

Prior art

Endler 1992; Endler & Basolo 1998; Moore et al. 1997; De Lisle et al. 2022; Maan & Seehausen 2012; Paaby & Rockman 2013; Wyles et al. 1983; Kirkpatrick 2009 and Blows & Walsh 2009 on G-matrix dimensionality (added); Fuller, Houle & Travis 2005, The American Naturalist 166(4):437–446, doi 10.1086/444443 (added); Hine & Blows 2006, Genetics 173(3):1135–1144, doi 10.1534/genetics.105.054627 (added); Cummings & Endler 2018 (added).

Test and evidence

Receiver-calibration selection (RCS) manuscript, §2–4 (P1, P2).

Falsifier

If the effective dimensionality of G across the task battery approaches the number of tasks, or selection through one receiver task produces no correlated response in unselected tasks sharing the parameter, RCS is wrong as formulated.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
