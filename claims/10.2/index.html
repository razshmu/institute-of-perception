10.2 Enrichment of receiver-calibration alleles in selectional-pleiotropy space

Claim

Receiver-calibration alleles are enriched in the high-magnitude, high-breadth region of the selectional-pleiotropy profile (A_g, B_g, K_g) relative to matched regulatory, neural, endocrine and developmental loci.

For preregistered, mutually exclusive contexts c with probabilities p_c and fitness effects s_g(c), magnitude is A_g = [Σ p_c s_g(c)²]^½. Breadth is B_g = exp[−KL(q_g‖p)] with q_gc = p_c s_g(c)²/A_g², so B_g = 1 when |s_g| is equal across contexts. Concordance is K_g = |Σ p_c s_g(c)| / Σ p_c|s_g(c)|. The profile stays a triple.

What is new

Paaby & Rockman (2013) define selectional pleiotropy, and the profile formulas were adopted from review. The claim here is the anatomical enrichment hypothesis for receiver loci, tested against matched controls.

Prior art

Paaby & Rockman 2013; Martin & Lenormand 2006; Wagner & Zhang 2011; Lande & Arnold 1983.

Test and evidence

RCS manuscript, Box 1 and P1; companion analysis of evolutionary leverage, §3 and §6.2.

Falsifier

After matching for effect size, expression breadth, network position and viability, receiver-calibration alleles show no higher B_g than matched non-receiver loci.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
