3.5 Structured propagation of calibration bias

Claim

Under independent multiplicative log-gains ρ_x, ρ_t, ρ_m on extent, duration and mass, a derived quantity q = ∏x_j^a_j carries log bias Σa_jρ_j: velocity ρ_x − ρ_t, kinetic energy ρ_m + 2(ρ_x − ρ_t), time-to-contact ρ_t.

Velocity bias cancels when spatial and temporal gains match. Kinetic energy amplifies the spatial–temporal difference because velocity enters squared. Time-to-contact computed as distance over velocity drops the spatial gain. The pattern across derived variables is a falsifiable fingerprint, and a homogeneous constraint Σc_iB(q_i) = 0 detects a uniform readout bias only when Σc_i ≠ 0.

What is new

Dimensional analysis gives the exponents; Lee’s tau shows optical time-to-contact is size-free. This claim applies exponent algebra to species-specific perceptual gains and predicts a joint pattern of cancellation and amplification across derived quantities.

Prior art

Dimensional analysis (Buckingham π theorem); Lee 1976; Buckingham & Goodale 2013.

Test and evidence

Derived in the companion physics notes and AMP §6; restated in the receiver-cascade map §4.

Falsifier

If all derived quantities shift by the same log amount, or velocity bias persists when independent estimates give ρ_x = ρ_t, the model fails.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
