14.4 Hierarchical adiabatic framing of nested gravitating systems

Claim

When ε = t_dyn(x)/t_evo(x+1) ≪ 1, a level-x gravitating system relaxes adiabatically inside the quasi-static potential of its host, and ε indexes this separation across star, galaxy, group and cluster levels.

The adiabatic approximation is textbook galactic dynamics. We add a level index and use ε as the diagnostic that decides where the separation holds. The Born-Oppenheimer name marks a structural analogy only.

What is new

Adiabatic invariance and adiabatic contraction are established (Binney & Tremaine 2008; Blumenthal et al. 1986). The new element is the level-indexed hierarchical framing with ε as a cross-level diagnostic.

Prior art

Born & Oppenheimer 1927; Binney & Tremaine 2008; Blumenthal et al. 1986; Gnedin et al. 2004; averaging methods in celestial mechanics.

Test and evidence

Measure ε per subsystem in simulations and test whether conservation of adiabatic invariants degrades as ε approaches one.

Falsifier

Adiabatic invariants of subsystems are poorly conserved in systems with small measured ε, or conserved equally well at ε near one.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
