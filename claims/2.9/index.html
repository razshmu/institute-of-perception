2.9 Encounter-equalisation test

Claim

Randomising settlement or equalising host, habitat, timing and encounter sequence tests sorting: matching or assortative mating that weakens while Os is unchanged was produced by non-random exposure.

The manipulation targets the Oi → E_exp link. It separates matching habitat choice and sorting from sensory drive and receiver bias. Persistence of matching under equalised encounters counts against sorting.

What is new

Matching habitat choice (Edelaar et al. 2008; Camacho et al. 2020) and public-information settlement (Doligez et al. 2002) describe sorting. This adds equalisation as the diagnostic intervention within the triangle.

Prior art

Edelaar et al. 2008; Doligez et al. 2002; Camacho et al. 2020.

Test and evidence

Proposed; applied in the JEB magic-trait Perspective draft.

Counts against

Matching persists undiminished under verified encounter randomisation in a system claimed to be sorting-driven.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.

Thesis 2: Chapter 3. Telling an environmental cause from a sensory one
