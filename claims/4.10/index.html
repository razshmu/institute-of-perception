4.10 Proto-body

Claim

The proto-body is a minimal self-model with three components: a boundary estimate, an internal-condition vector and a controllability map.

The proto-body is the interface where outward perceptual categories meet inward regulation. It carries only what regulation needs. Each component is measurable: boundary by ownership and localization, internal condition by interoceptive state, controllability by reafference.

What is new

Damasio’s proto-self is a neural map of body state. The proto-body adds the boundary estimate and the controllability map as separate components, and defines it at the body-world partition rather than in neural anatomy.

Prior art

Damasio 1999; Metzinger 2003; Seth 2013.

Test and evidence

Dissociate the three components: perturb controllability (delayed feedback) with boundary and interoceptive state held fixed, and the reverse.

Counts against

Every manipulation moves all three components together.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
