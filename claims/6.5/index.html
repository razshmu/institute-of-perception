6.5 Octopus-arm and haltere programme

Claim

In octopus arms, discrete-joint models are compared with smooth-curvature models on held-out reaching kinematics; in Drosophila halteres, a two-field model of campaniform sensilla is compared with a smooth field and genotype-level q is tested against flight performance.

Haltere q is estimated across genetic lines and regressed on flight performance after controlling total sensory gain and haltere kinematics. Experimental selection for manoeuvrability versus stability runs only if q is heritable.

What is new

Octopus quasi-joints are known; this applies q and genotype-level selection tests to them and to halteres.

Prior art

Dickinson 1999; Sumbre et al. 2005; Mackay et al. 2012 (DGRP).

Test and evidence

Design only.

Counts against

Two-region models do not outperform smooth models, or haltere q does not predict flight performance.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.

Thesis 6: Chapter 7. When one limb counts as many
