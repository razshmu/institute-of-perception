4.2 Partition precedes metric

Claim

A distinction must be stabilized (EFS) before selection can tune metric equivalence within it (Adaptive Metric Perception).

EFS and Adaptive Metric Perception are separable theories related by dependency. A metric along a dimension presupposes that the dimension is drawn. The reverse does not hold: a partition needs no metric.

What is new

Orders partition before metric in an evolutionary account and so links the author’s two programmes; measurement theory orders nominal before metric scales but not as an evolutionary sequence.

Prior art

Stevens 1946; Krantz et al. 1971.

Test and evidence

Comparative: lineages with metric tuning along a dimension must show the partition for that dimension; the converse need not hold.

Falsifier

A lineage shows selected metric equivalence along a dimension it does not partition.

An outcome that contradicts the claim as stated.
