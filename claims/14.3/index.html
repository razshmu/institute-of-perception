14.3 Cosmic time as a stack of measurement layers

Claim

Humanity’s measured span of cosmic time grew because instruments added measurement layers, and telescopes extend the celestial-cue extraction that animals already use for navigation.

Nocturnal birds and dung beetles extract orientation cues from the sky. Human astronomy extends the same cue extraction with instruments and inference. Each added layer (parallax, standard candles, redshift, the CMB within a fitted cosmological model) extended the time span that can be inferred.

What is new

History of science documents instrument-driven expansion of the time scale. The claim adds the biological precedent of animal celestial navigation and places both under one normalisation operator (14.2).

Prior art

Emlen 1967; Dacke et al. 2013; Davis & Lineweaver 2004; Chang 2004; Gibbon 1977; Healy et al. 2013.

Test and evidence

Historical reconstruction linking each extension of inferred cosmic age to a new measurement layer.

Counts against

A documented extension of inferred cosmic age that came from theory alone, with no new measurement layer.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
