12.7 Measurement noise as access diagnostic

Claim

Residual measurement error that covaries with features of the access system (observer, instrument, laboratory, calibration state or pipeline) is evidence about where mediation enters a result, and it is modeled as such rather than averaged away.

Write a measurement as Y = μ(a) + β_P + γ_P·Z + ε, where Z holds access variables. Residuals that are independent, centred and shrink with repetition support the stability of the procedure. Residuals that carry information about Z identify the access components that shape the result. Objectivity then grows by modeling, distributing, calibrating and publicly controlling those components.

What is new

Metrology already partitions error by source: gauge R&R, ISO 5725 interlaboratory trials, random-effects models of observer and laboratory, and batch-effect modeling. The claim adds an epistemological reading inside the access account: structured error is data about the access system.

Prior art

ISO 5725; gauge R&R; JCGM GUM 2008; Mayo 1996; Leek et al. 2010.

Test and evidence

Formalization Section 0707, Proposition 5 and section 1.14.

Counts against

In some measurement domain, residuals correlate with access variables, yet modeling that correlation never changes calibration, attribution or the stated result.

An outcome that weakens or narrows the claim without contradicting it.
