Where the body ends

The question

Every claim so far has assumed a boundary: there is an organism, and there is an environment, and signals cross between them. That boundary looks like a fact of anatomy. A tool held in the hand is not part of the hand.

Yet the boundary an animal operates with is not the anatomical one. It shifts with training, it breaks under delay, and it does not always coincide with the skin. The question is whether that operational partition is itself an evolved achievement — something built, maintained and capable of being selected on.

What the thesis says

Experiential Field Selection locates the target of selection carefully, and the care is the substance of the claim. Selection acts on the sensory, bodily, interoceptive and sensorimotor architecture that draws the body-world partition. It does not act on experience, and it does not act on the partition itself. Architecture is heritable and variable; a partition is an output.

This ordering has a consequence for the previous chapter. A distinction has to be stabilised before selection can tune the metric within it. You cannot be selected for judging distances along a dimension you do not yet separate out. The implied falsifier is one line: a lineage showing selected metric equivalence along a dimension it does not partition would break the ordering.

The account of where the partition comes from is deliberately unromantic. It begins with a membrane, chemotaxis and reafference — the correlation between an action and its sensory consequences — and it begins before there is any perceiving subject. Before the partition is drawn, the cue stream is not yet sorted into body, object, route, threat and background. Those categories are the product, not the starting point.

Reafference also supplies the criterion for what counts as self. The self-cluster is the part of the sensory stream carrying the highest joint mutual information with action-contingent feedback and interoceptive state. This is a measurable quantity rather than a definition, and it comes with its own falsifier: if the privileged cluster tracks stimulus salience rather than contingency, the account is wrong about what makes it privileged.

The thesis also proposes a mechanism specific enough to be checked in a laboratory. Signals that can be integrated within an organism’s multisensory temporal binding window are assigned to the body. If that is right, baseline temporal acuity should predict the visuomotor delay at which ownership of a limb breaks down, and people with extensive tool experience should tolerate longer delays.

A worked example

The worked example is not an anecdote but a prediction about coupling, and it is the sharpest test of the thesis.

Suppose the partition is one achievement rather than a collection of separate judgements. Changing the receiver state or architecture under a matched cue field should then reorganise a whole family of behaviours together. Those are: what counts as within reach, the threshold for a defensive response, the route taken, the choice of shelter, the spacing kept from others, and contact near the body. Not one of these, and not each on its own schedule.

This is a strong prediction, and it is stated with the statistical test that would defeat it. The null is that these categories are conditionally independent once measured internal state, detection ability and motor capacity are accounted for. If the behaviours come apart under that control, no single partition is being reorganised. That happens when the apparent coupling is explained by a shared latent state, or by differences in what the animal can detect or do. The thesis would then be describing a correlation rather than a structure.

A second condition is easy to miss: the loadings should differ across niches. A pattern that looks identical in every ecological setting is more likely a property of the measurement than of the animal.

What would refute it

Four falsifiers are stated, and they attack different joints of the argument.

Against the ordering: a lineage with selected metric equivalence along an unpartitioned dimension.

Against the origin account: body-object distinctions appearing in lineages that lack the reafferent control the account places before them. This is a comparative claim about who has what, and it can be checked.

Against the mechanism: no correlation between baseline temporal acuity and the delay at which ownership breaks, and no extension of delay tolerance in tool experts.

Against the criterion for self: a privileged cluster that tracks salience rather than contingency.

Stars regulate themselves, and so do organisms. The distinction offered is that a star’s feedback preserves a balance of forces, while an organism’s feedback preserves the conditions without which the regulating process itself would stop. The falsifier is a non-living system whose feedback preserves the conditions of its own regulating process in that same closed way. Whether such a system exists is a real and open question.

A term introduced here

The proto-body is the minimal version of what this chapter describes: a self-model with three components — a boundary estimate, a vector of internal conditions, and a map of what the system can control.

It is the smallest thing that can be said to have an inside. Everything in later chapters that depends on there being a receiver depends on something at least this complex having been achieved first.

The claims this chapter rests on

  • 4.1
    Experiential Field Selection (EFS) Central thesis

    Selection acts on the sensory, bodily, interoceptive and sensorimotor architecture that draws the operational body-world partition, not on experience or the partition itself.

  • 4.2
    Partition precedes metric Specific extension

    A distinction must be stabilized (EFS) before selection can tune metric equivalence within it (Adaptive Metric Perception).

  • 4.3
    Pre-partition and membrane-first origin Specific extension

    The partition originates in a membrane plus chemotaxis and reafference, before any perceiving subject; before it is drawn, the cue stream is not sorted for the organism into body, object, route, threat or background.

  • 4.5
    Body-world boundary set by a temporal binding window Testable hypothesis

    Signals integrable within the organism’s multisensory temporal binding window are assigned to the body, so baseline temporal acuity predicts the visuomotor delay at which body ownership breaks.

  • 4.6
    Coupled repartitioning across action categories Testable hypothesis

    Under a matched cue field, a change in receiver state or architecture reorganizes reachability, defensive threshold, route, shelter, social spacing and body-near contact together, as one partition not reducible to a single latent state.

  • 4.10
    Proto-body Terminological proposal

    The proto-body is a minimal self-model with three components: a boundary estimate, an internal-condition vector and a controllability map.
